# Printing and reading

Published articles have a **Print** button.

<figure><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Boy4K/bk-ww-docs/main/assets/news-print.jpg" alt="Choose front page or newspaper"><figcaption><p>Choose front page or newspaper</p></figcaption></figure>

| Type | Item | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Front page for walls** | `news_poster` | stick it like a WANTED poster; on the wall, **Read the newspaper** opens the full article |
| **Newspaper to read** | `newspaper` | use it from the inventory to read the article; give or sell it to other players |

Newspapers always show the current article text, so a long article is readable even if the front page shows only its start.
